## Considered options

| Choice | Reason |
|---|---|
| One full local/CI quality policy, with partial fast pre-commit | Selected: agents receive the same blocking quality standard before pushing; CI additionally establishes native/forge/approval evidence. A missing local scanner cannot count as full success |
| Independent admission and authoring/worker VM platform | Earlier interview selections were explicitly superseded. They cost a different operational/authority project and are not promised follow-ups or v0 prerequisites |
| Universal structural hard caps or a composite quality score | Rejected: tool defaults are review signals, not demonstrated universal defect boundaries. Calibrate cohorts and useful cohesive changes before separately reviewed promotion |
| Aggregate mutation non-regression alone | Insufficient for critical code: gains elsewhere can hide a lost assertion or weak first initialization. Add reviewed critical mappings/survivor treatment without inventing a universal mutation floor |
| Post-merge-only detection of known selection/duplication gaps | Superseded: required selection must be conservative, duplication must cover the approved corpus before merge, and the first confirmed miss/stale pass quarantines the implicated optimization |
| Require a whole-bootstrap mutation baseline before ticket 01 | Replaced by a bounded campaign over all declared critical contracts in actual operational code, plus runtime/native integration proof. Six entire critical modules retained roughly 84% of the observed mutant population; selecting fewer filenames alone would not buy the intended reduction. Unselected faults remain an explicit assurance limit |
| Product-only or advisory PR mutation | Declined for Superman's amended contract: operational gate changes also matter. Fresh risk-affected scopes and conservative fallback retain blocking comparable-scope non-regression without rerunning every critical module on unrelated PRs |
| An unconditional short PR deadline | Replaced by explicit normal and slow pre-merge classes. Large required scopes may take longer, but must complete before merge. Slow work remains visible in end-to-end cost rather than being hidden behind a fast-lane statistic |
| Full mutation nightly on every platform | Replaced by weekly/release broad mutation on approved Linux-x64, with proven delegation and native boundary fault controls. This accepts delayed discovery outside PR scope and the maintenance cost of platform classification. The final interview restored weekly audits and the fourteen-day freshness stop after a withdrawn release-only choice |
| Reject every mutant-induced Timeout | Refined: positively attributed nontermination can be a resolved temporal fault while raw Timeout and conservative scoring remain intact. Host/setup ambiguity still invalidates evidence; correct loops should not be rewritten merely to erase a tool status |
| Remove equivalents from the score denominator | Declined. Reviewed semantic equivalence changes a specific blocking disposition, not raw outcomes or score meaning. A runner-discovery gap is not equivalence |

## Consequences

Keep Archgate, Biome, primary tsrs plus supplemental complete Deno checking, Vitest, Stryker, jscpd, Knip and Trivy. Deno runs owned operational code; real Node/pnpm remain retained-tool or bounded measurement hosts. No overlapping suite, runtime fallback, dependency/pin change or automatic tool replacement is implied. Explicit correctness errors, separate structural advisories, complete four-metric coverage and critical behavioral/mutation evidence must be fixture-proved; a recommended preset or tool exit alone establishes none of these additions.

Policy-defined applicability is explicit; missing tools, ignored failures, skipped required work and incomplete/stale evidence cannot satisfy the full quality result. Exact-input reuse remains conditional for eligible non-mutation gates, with fresh critical/security/integrity checks and applicable uncached nightly evidence. Required mutation is fresh at its own declared scope/cadence; an incremental report is not acceptance. Protected inputs, scopes, severities and dispositions remain Gate 1-governed, not agent-authored waivers. Known invalid evidence blocks despite the accepted same-runtime reporter/oracle risks.

The retained independent overall coverage floors/target and no per-file/automatic ratchet decision stay in ADR 0014. Ordinary coverage cannot establish mutation discovery, activation or assertion sensitivity. Mutation retains Killed/total and equivalent-scope comparisons. Valid NoCoverage is undetected; broken declared discovery/activation invalidates the measurement. In the deliberately bounded mandatory critical scope, first and subsequent measurements must resolve required findings through meaningful kills, reviewed semantic equivalence or a proven applicable termination fault; an aggregate gain or a known blind spot cannot admit critical debt.

Scope-bound baseline records in spec §5.3 separate bounded, product and broad measurements. The existing scalar/hash format needs a protected reader migration: policy identity is not a complete source/test/runtime fingerprint, and a narrowed result cannot tighten a full baseline. The public gate must read back and validate actual evidence/dispositions. Weekly/release audit consequences and budgets live in §5.4, useful-outcome/cost pilot criteria in §13 and the closed pre-ticket-01 mutation delta in §17. These criteria observe existing process/report seams, not general correctness of a new measurement harness (ADR 0018).

The accepted local Deno bridge pilot retains its local fixture scope. Historical failed G6 evidence retains its failure, even where its diagnostic ratio matches a committed scalar or a later policy accepts a new timeout disposition. The baseline's existence, a changed hash or a source label is not retroactive acceptance. The current evaluator governs until the replacement is evidenced and approved.

The [concurrent run's terminal receipt](../../.scratch/superman-v0/gate1-preparation.md#terminal-result-and-integration--2026-11-09) illustrates the distinction: its G6 measurement passed while the receipt failed input integrity because planning edits shared its checkout. Preserve both outcomes. An accepted baseline needs valid source-bound evidence, so §5.3/M05 explicitly reject that failed receipt even when its subgate passed.

This is confirmed design with **unproved activation**, not human Gate 1 approval, ticket admission or a green CI claim. Existing documents and executable work retain their provenance; the original quality synthesis edited no tickets. The later [combined reconciliation](../../.scratch/superman-v0/code-quality-planning.md) aligns only its separately approved planning/backlog delta and records the completed SUP-005 slice without upgrading it to Deno execution. Actual Deno/measurement/native-platform/tool/environment/host-authority evidence remains outstanding under §17. André is accountable for policy, calibration and recovery until explicit bounded delegation.
