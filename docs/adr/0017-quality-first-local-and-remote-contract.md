# One quality contract locally and remotely, without a new admission platform

André confirmed [the quality interview's current contract and Q35](../../.scratch/superman-v0/code-quality-amendment.md) on 2026-11-01. U2/Q32 select lean repository-owned CI: strengthen observable quality and fail-closed evidence accounting with the retained tools, rather than make v0 depend on a new external controller/App, VM platform, credential broker or policy-generation rollout. The existing PR-owned-checker/human-review and identity limits remain (ADRs 0001/0005); bounded dynamic evidence is not tamper-proof correctness proof. Spec §5.4 owns acceptance, §5.7 optimization/recovery, §8 existing self-hosted execution and §17 readiness.

## Considered options

| Choice | Reason |
|---|---|
| One full local/CI quality policy, with partial fast pre-commit | Selected: agents receive the same blocking quality standard before pushing; CI additionally establishes native/forge/approval evidence. A missing local scanner cannot count as full success |
| Independent admission and authoring/worker VM platform | Earlier interview selections were explicitly superseded. They cost a different operational/authority project and are not promised follow-ups or v0 prerequisites |
| Universal structural hard caps or a composite quality score | Rejected: tool defaults are review signals, not demonstrated universal defect boundaries. Calibrate cohorts and useful cohesive changes before separately reviewed promotion |
| Aggregate mutation non-regression alone | Insufficient for critical code: gains elsewhere can hide a lost assertion or weak first initialization. Add reviewed critical mappings/survivor treatment without inventing a universal mutation floor |
| Post-merge-only detection of known selection/duplication gaps | Superseded: required selection must be conservative, duplication must cover the approved corpus before merge, and the first confirmed miss/stale pass quarantines the implicated optimization |

## Consequences

Keep Archgate, Biome, primary tsrs plus supplemental complete Deno checking, Vitest, Stryker, jscpd, Knip and Trivy. Deno runs owned operational code; real Node/pnpm remain retained-tool or bounded measurement hosts. No overlapping suite, runtime fallback, dependency/pin change or automatic tool replacement is implied. Explicit correctness errors, separate structural advisories, complete four-metric coverage and critical behavioral/mutation evidence must be fixture-proved; a recommended preset or tool exit alone establishes none of these additions.

Policy-defined applicability is explicit; missing tools, ignored failures, skipped required work and incomplete/stale evidence cannot satisfy the full quality result. Exact-input reuse remains conditional, with fresh critical/security/integrity checks and uncached full nightly evidence. Protected inputs, scopes, severities and suppression semantics remain Gate 1-governed, not agent-authored waivers. Known invalid evidence blocks despite the accepted same-runtime reporter/oracle risks.

The retained independent overall coverage floors/target and no per-file/automatic ratchet decision stay in ADR 0014. Mutation preserves conservative outcome accounting and equivalent-scope comparisons; critical first measurements need André's reviewed survivor treatment. No first baseline is fabricated and no aggregate improvement excuses an unresolved introduced critical survivor. These contracts include owned enforcement-core code, not a new controller component.

This is confirmed design with **unproved activation**, not human Gate 1 approval, ticket admission or a green CI claim. Existing documents and executable work retain their provenance; the original quality synthesis edited no tickets. The later [combined reconciliation](../../.scratch/superman-v0/code-quality-planning.md) aligns only its separately approved planning/backlog delta and records the completed SUP-005 slice without upgrading it to Deno execution. Actual Deno/measurement/native-platform/tool/environment/host-authority evidence remains outstanding under §17. André is accountable for policy, calibration and recovery until explicit bounded delegation.
